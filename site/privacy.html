Robin privacy policy

Last updated 15 October 2026

Robin helps parents of babies ask family and friends for a hand, plan events, chat and keep memories. Robin is a student project. It is available for a short time for testing and assessment, and will then be removed along with all of its data.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, password (stored only in scrambled form) and time zone.
  • Family details: the families you belong to, your role in each (owner, parent or helper) and an optional name the family uses for you, such as "Nana".
  • What you share: help requests and events, replies, messages, and photos and videos you add to chats or Memories, with their captions and dates.
  • Notifications: your notification choices and, if you allow notifications, a device token used only to deliver them.

Robin does not collect your location, contacts, advertising ID or any information about children beyond what parents choose to share in photos and messages. There are no ads and no third-party analytics.

How it is used

Only to run Robin: to sign you in, show your family's requests, calendar, chats and memories to the right people, and send you notifications you've asked for. We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Only people in your family on Robin, and only what is shared with them. Chats are seen only by the people in that chat. Memories can be marked "parents only". Photos and videos are stored privately and open only through short-lived links for people allowed to see them.

Where it is stored

Robin's data is stored by Supabase, a hosting provider, with access rules that stop anyone outside your family from reading it. Data is encrypted in transit.

Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app under You & family → Privacy & account → Delete my account, or by following the steps on our account deletion page. This removes your profile, your uploads and your access to every family. Messages you sent remain in chats as "Former member" so conversations still make sense to others.

When the testing period ends, the whole service and all data in it will be deleted.

Children

Robin is for adults. Children do not have accounts. Parents decide which photos of their children to share, and only with their own family.
