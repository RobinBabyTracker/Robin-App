Delete your Robin account

In the app

  1. Open Robin and tap your picture at the top right.
  2. Tap Privacy & account.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

By email

If you can't open the app, email robin.babytracker@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Robin account". We'll delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, email address and sign-in details.
  • Photos and videos you uploaded, and memories you added.
  • Your membership of every family, your notification settings and device tokens.
  • Requests you organised that haven't happened yet are cancelled.

What is kept

Messages you sent stay in the chat, shown as "Former member", so the conversation still makes sense to others. Nothing else is kept. When Robin's testing period ends, all remaining data is deleted.
